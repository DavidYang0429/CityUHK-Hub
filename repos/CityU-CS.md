香港城市大学 BSc CS 课程笔记与项目汇总。为未来的学生提供全面的资源，包括课程笔记、复习总结、练习题和项目示例，帮助他们更好地理解课程内容并为考试做好准备。作者专业 GPA 4.17/4.3（年级排名 1/190），希望通过分享自己的学习经验和资源，帮助更多的同学取得优异成绩。

A detailed collection of notes and course projects for the BSc Computer Science program at City University of Hong Kong, providing a comprehensive resource for future students. Offers detailed notes, review summaries, exercises, and project examples for each course, helping students better understand the course content and prepare for exams. The author graduated with a major GPA of 4.17/4.3 (ranked 1/190 in the cohort) and hopes to share his learning experience and resources to help more students achieve academic success.

## 特点

- 覆盖 BSc CS 课程中的几乎所有科目，分为专业核心、专业选修、大学要求和通识课程
- 涵盖广泛的主题，包括编程、数据结构与算法、软件工程、人工智能等
- 提供每门课程的详细笔记、复习总结、练习题和项目示例
- 包含最新课程网站和资源的链接，确保学生能够获取最新信息
- 提供专业选修课程的选择建议，以及对部分课程的难度、工作量和实用性评分，帮助学生在选课时做出明智的决策

## Features

- Covering almost all courses in the BSc CS program, categorized into major core, major elective, university requirement, and general education courses
- Covering a wide range of topics, including programming, data structures and algorithms, software engineering, artificial intelligence, and more
- Providing detailed notes, review summaries, exercises, and project examples for each course
- Including links to the latest course websites and resources, ensuring students have access to up-to-date information
- Offering recommendations for selecting major electives as well as rating of some courses based on difficulty, workload, and usefulness, helping students make informed decisions about their course selection
